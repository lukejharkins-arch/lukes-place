# Social content and community plan

**Channels:** Instagram (primary), TikTok, YouTube Shorts, email newsletter (consented waitlist), rules-compliant participation in collector communities on Reddit and Facebook. No posting happens without founder approval; nothing is scheduled yet.

## Weekly rhythm (pre-launch, 4 posts a week)
| Day | Series | Era | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Vault Notes**: one construction detail | OG | "Why the snaps sit on a placket" (macro video) |
| Wed | **Paper Trail**: how one item was authenticated | Current | Letter, tags, custody, classification in 20 seconds |
| Fri | **Aussies On Court**: a sourced Australian story | Both | "Seven Australians have won an NBA title" with sources in the caption |
| Sat | **Wanted Wall**: a collector's wanted post (with permission) | Both | "Who has 1996-97 Bulls warmup pants in XL?" |

Monthly: one collector spotlight interview; one "Team issue or retail?" quiz that teaches the tag differences.

## Era-specific accounts of the same brand
OG posts use cream grounds, grain and oversized type; Current posts use ink, lime and clean product shots. Same handle, same voice; the grid alternates eras.

## Australian angle
Saint Mary's Gaels pipeline (Mills, Dellavedova, Landale, Ducas), the 2026-27 Australian NBA contingent, and Australian champions (Longley, Gaze, Mills, Baynes, Bogut, Dellavedova, White). Every fact in a caption carries a source in our register first.

## Community features in the product
- **Wanted board** (live, moderated): collectors post what they're hunting; the Gear Hunter matches posts to leads.
- **Wishlists** (live): saved exhibits signal demand by team and era.
- **Consignment intake** (live): applications with a preliminary evidence assessment.
- **Collector Exchange** (planned): peer listings only after seller verification tiers, buyer protection and legal review.
- **Locker Room nights** (planned): meet-ups with Australian collector groups.

## KPIs (to be measured, none claimed yet)
Waitlist sign-ups with consent · wanted posts · consignment applications · saves per post · comments asking "how do I consign?".
