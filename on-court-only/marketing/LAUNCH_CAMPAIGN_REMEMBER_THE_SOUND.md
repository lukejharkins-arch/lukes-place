# REMEMBER THE SOUND? | Tearaway launch campaign

**Goal:** make tearaway warmup pants the thing people know ON COURT ONLY for, and fill the waitlist before the first 30-piece drop.
**Measured by:** waitlist sign-ups with consent, wanted posts, consignment applications, sell-through of the first drop. No paid media until founder approval.

## The idea
Everyone who watched 1990s and 2000s basketball remembers the rip of warmups coming off at the scorer's table. We own that sound: recorded from our own garments, never from broadcasts.

## Phases
| Phase | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Tease | Weeks -6 to -4 | Black screen, one SNAP, "Remember the sound?" Link to the waitlist. Founder posts "what we're building and why". |
| 2. Teach | Weeks -4 to -1 | Tearaway Vault content: construction guide, team issue versus retail, how we authenticate. One real item documented end to end, from tags to custody. |
| 3. Drop | Week 0 | First 30 verified pieces. Waitlist gets 48 hours of early access. Every piece shows its full provenance. |
| 4. Community | Weeks +1 to +8 | Collector stories, the wanted board, consignment intake. |

## Authentic demonstrations (instead of quality claims)
- **The snap test:** macro video of a real pair's snap run, counting the snaps, filmed on a plain background.
- **Tag table:** a team-issue tag beside a retail tag from the same season, with only verified differences called out.
- **The paper trail:** a 20-second walk through one item's evidence: receipt or letter, custody steps, classification.
- **Size reality:** a pro length tag (e.g. XL+2) on a model, explaining what it means.
Rule: say "this pair" and show it; never "team issue is always better".

## Guardrails
Original footage and sound only; no league, team or manufacturer logos in ads (they may appear on the genuine item being photographed); no player likeness in ads; scarcity claims must be true; email only to consented contacts (Spam Act 2003).
