## IDENTITY & VISION

The distinctive product is genuine, team-issued on-court basketball apparel that almost never reaches conventional retail: snap-side tearaway pants, shooting shirts, game warmups, tournament/Four Final sets, player-issued footwear and historically significant jerseys. Player-worn/game-worn is the higher-priced, separately evidenced premium vault. Spotlight fans who recognise a season by its warmup design. Statement: **Where basketball love meets authentic on-court wear.** Supporting: **Remember the sound?**, **The gear you saw. The gear you couldn't get.**, **Wear the game, not just the logo.** Avoid denigrating regular fans.

## COMMERCE AND TRUST CLASSIFICATION
A TEAM-ISSUED = evidenced produced/supplied by team for team use. B PLAYER-ISSUED = documented allocation to specific athlete, not proof of wear. C PLAYER-WORN/GAME-WORN = explicit wear proof and precise game/practice/event context. D VINTAGE TEAM ISSUE = historically team-issued with provenance. E KICKS = PE/sample/retail/game-worn distinctly recorded. F RETAIL = separate clearly labelled non-core community area. G UNVERIFIED = not eligible for a core product listing. Track independent authenticator/certificates, custody chain, manufacturer tags, internal codes, seller history, photos, match evidence and rights.
