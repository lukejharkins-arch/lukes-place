# Founder decisions and non-negotiables

1. Brand: ON COURT ONLY. Working name only; check availability and legal review.
2. Hybrid model: owned + consignment + permissioned partner listings.
3. Inventory acquisition budget must remain **below AUD 2,000**; planning allocation: owned tearaways A$850; other pieces A$450; freight/import buffer A$350; authentication/prep A$150; reserve A$150 (A$1,950 total). This is a planning envelope, not authorisation to spend. Platform and creative spend need separate approval.
4. Launch with **30 controlled genuine items**, planning mix 8 owned, 17 signed consignments, 5 supply-partner items with written listing authority. Adjust based on evidence.
5. Distinguish TEAM-ISSUED / PLAYER-ISSUED / PLAYER-WORN / GAME-WORN / RETAIL / UNVERIFIED. No promotion of unsupported claim.
6. Search everywhere legally: official sources, eBay, Depop, Vinted, Grailed, Facebook Marketplace, Gumtree, thrift/estate/charity shops, surplus auctions, vintage dealers, consignment, collector networks, garage sales, wholesale, other country-specific resale platforms. Under-described and undervalued listings are an opportunity, but verify before resale.
7. Prioritise high-engagement teams and stories (Finals, championships, Kobe/Penny/Steph eras, Australian connections, NCAA Final Four) using evidence, not intuition alone.
8. Two modes: OG Archive: era-inspired 1990s/2000s graphic vocabulary; Current Court: contemporary restrained basketball streetwear. Both original branded design, no implied NBA/NCAA endorsement.
9. Players and manufacturers can be named factually in listings for genuine goods; manufacturer/team logos appearing on actual item photos are part of documenting that genuine item. Separate usage of protected badges in site identity, ads, creative overlays, archival action photos or endorsement claims requires rights review. Do not assume universal permission.
10. Daily target 20 A-grade opportunities, fortnightly follow-up drafts, ongoing relationship building only with real scheduling/tools and approvals.
