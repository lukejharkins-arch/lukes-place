# Scoring, authenticity, controls and release gate

## Deal scoring (100 points)
- Provenance/authentication evidence 30 (gate: weak = not A)
- Landed economics and sold comparable 25
- Expected engagement/team-season-story 15
- Rarity and difficulty finding equivalent 10
- Condition/sizing/desirability 10
- Reseller reliability/fulfilment/export 10
A candidate requires >=80/100 plus no unresolved counterfeit/ownership/export issues. 60–79 investigate B; <60 C. Confidence score is not independent authentication.

## Authentication hierarchy
1. Original team/official partner provenance and item-level records.
2. Reputable independent authentication with independently verifiable reference IDs.
3. Credible chain of custody plus detailed internal construction evidence.
4. Player/team in-use photo design comparisons (do not equate same design to same physical item).
5. Seller claims only = insufficient team-issued proof.
Labels: TEAM-ISSUED / PLAYER-ISSUED / PLAYER-WORN / GAME-WORN / VINTAGE RETAIL / RARE RETAIL KICKS / UNVERIFIED. Premium wear requires event-specific evidence; no inferred photo matches.

## Budget
Strictly <A$2,000 in approved acquisition commitments inclusive of acquisition-related shipping/import. Planning only: $850 tearaways, $450 other, $350 freight/import, $150 authenticating/prep, $150 reserve = $1,950. Do not spend without founder approval; quote-only estimates are not actual costs. Ecom operating costs are a separate approval.

## Launch gate
Target 30 controlled genuine stock units: 8 owned, 17 signed consignment and 5 authorised partner controlled. For each verify rights to sell, custody/fulfilment, evidence and honest grading, actual item photographs, licensed/reference media status, condition, price, SKU, secure checkout, shipping, returns, legal policies. Do NOT count internet wishlists or fictitious items. Test payment flows before live launch. Seller-provided goods need written agreements and dispute procedures.

## Australian rights / claims
Genuine items may be described accurately with team, player, manufacturer names and photographed as physical items; right to use logos/archival pictures in ads, graphics and brand identity may need different permissions. Obtain Australian trademark/consumer-law/IP advice before launch. Product references should state 'Independent reseller, not affiliated with or endorsed by the NBA, NCAA, teams or manufacturers' where accurate; disclaimer does not cure infringement.
