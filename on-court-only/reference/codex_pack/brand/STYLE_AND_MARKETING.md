# ON COURT ONLY: two-era original design system

## Brand core
ON COURT ONLY in proprietary letter styling, bold athletic attitude, equipment-room serial accents, tearaway snap motif, no imitation of NBA shield/team crests/Nike swoosh. 'Where basketball love meets authentic on-court wear.' 'Remember the sound?'. Real gear rather than mainstream sameness. Use official team/player/manufacturer names factually for genuine items and image of the physical item with its applied labels; don't place league/team marks in own identity or imply endorsement. Commercial archival/action photos require licensed rights. Consult IP counsel before ads.

## Mode 1: OG ARCHIVE (circa 1996–2010)
Nostalgia: saturated electric purple, teal, burnt orange and cream with charcoal; era-evocative oversized block headers, print-like collage textures, seam stitching and visible side-snap dots. Distressed textures are ORIGINAL, no copied retro team artwork, no fake licences. Historic equipment product cards should carry big season tags and 'TEAM ISSUE / ERA / VERIFIED PROVENANCE'. Human editorial warmth. Primary content: Penny Magic aesthetic, Bulls dynasty, Kobe-era Lakers, early NBA warmups, old-school NCAA events. Brand visuals can evoke period but avoid exact copyrighted team trade dress.

## Mode 2: CURRENT COURT (2015–present)
Luxury sporting-tech: ink #11151B, chalk #F8F5EB, court-lime #C1ED57, understated steel. Bold condensed visual rhythm, product evidence closeups, large season text, crisp minimal cards, subtle motion showing snap mechanism. Championship-specific drop pages and 2026 current-season archive, validated events only. Primary content: Finals vault, City Edition-era, modern college Final Four, players in the tunnel and game warmups (licensed imagery).

## Hero campaign: THE TEARAWAY REVIVAL
Hook: 'REMEMBER THE SOUND?' Show genuine snap sides, closeups and texture, team issue vs retail features where verified. Original sonic snap effect. 15-sec: closeup snap -> old-school warmup silhouette -> actual item evidence -> 'Gear you saw. Gear you couldn't get.' 30-sec: nostalgia + manufacturing changes + provenance and new arrivals. Don't claim all retail are low-grade or all team issue are unique.

## The Other Side of the Glass story
Fictional young player receives college shirts and shoes, other kids watch from a glass divide; dorm then pro gear, kids now adult fans; gear returns via lawful source, item verified and crosses into fan collection. Never suggest real identifiable athletes disregard gear; develop screenplay, 15/30/60/90 cuts, original cinematic performance and rights-aware imagery.

## Player image discipline
Every item should show side-by-side: actual owned/consigned item photo + right-side authorised archive photo of same design, or honest linked/text reference if rights absent. Labels: EXACT ITEM PHOTO-MATCHED (strong event/item proof); SAME DESIGN SHOWN IN USE (not proof of wear); ERA REFERENCE; REFERENCE PENDING. Don't fetch someone else's photo and republish it without usage rights.

## Social
Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, controlled Facebook/Reddit community participation, email newsletter, collector interviews, 'wanted' board. Separate OG nostalgia and Current Court story reels; Australian NCAA/NBA lineage makes differentiator. Scarcity claims evidence-based. Promote real second-hand goods using accurate brand/player/team descriptions subject to applicable rights.
