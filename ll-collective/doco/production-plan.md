# Documentary and social production plan

Produced in house. No filming. Founder voices on audio, AI-generated visuals.

## Capture pipeline

1. **Audio interviews.** Harbour sends questions before each session. Record on a phone voice memo or a meeting recorder (Granola is already connected and can hold transcripts). Transcripts go to `doco/interviews/`.
2. **Story log.** Harbour logs each key moment as it happens (`story-log.md`).
3. **Evidence archive.** Metric snapshots, spend, experiment results and screenshots go to `doco/evidence/`, dated.
4. **Monthly gap check.** Harbour updates the story outline and lists the moments, interviews and visuals still missing.

## Working structure (to be revised)

- Act 1, the premise (Nov 2026 to early 2027): the charter, the research, the first paid test.
- Act 2, the build (2027 prep year): proving demand, automating, building the audience.
- Act 3, the race (1 Dec 2027 to 30 Nov 2028): the $500k clock.
- Ending: the result, whatever it is.

## Interview bank: kickoff session

1. Why now, and why the two of you?
2. What does $500k actually change for you?
3. Why hand the running of it to an AI?
4. What are you most sceptical about?
5. What would make you call it off?
6. How will you know it worked, even if the number isn't hit?

## Social

Channels chosen after the opportunity is selected, to match where the buyer is. Harbour drafts all posts; founders approve each one before it goes live. Content calendar to follow in `doco/social-calendar.md`.

## Visual style

To be chosen by the founders. Harbour will present three style options with sample frames before production starts.
