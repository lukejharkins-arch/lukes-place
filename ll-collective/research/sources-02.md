# Sources 02: Growth and distribution evidence base

Compiled by Wren (Growth and Distribution specialist) for Harbour, 18 Nov 2026. All accessed 18 Nov 2026 via web search.

**Access caveat.** This session could not open acma.gov.au, legislation.gov.au, austlii.edu.au or holmanwebb.com.au directly (network allowlist). Figures from those sites come from search-engine extracts of the pages, not a full read. Items marked **VERIFY** should be checked against the primary page before any decision that relies on them.

**Reliability grades.** A = primary source (regulator, statute, platform's own docs or pricing, company filing). B = large-sample study or a reputable trade publication reporting one. C = vendor or agency blog with a disclosed dataset. D = anecdote, forum post or undisclosed method.
