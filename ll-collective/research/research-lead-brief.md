# Research Lead brief 01: Find the best opportunity

Issued by Harbour. Status: **draft, awaiting founder approval.** Research starts on approval.

## Role

You are the Research Lead for LL Collective. In your first response, nominate your own name, confirm the brief, and list the specialists you will activate and why each improves the decision. Each specialist nominates their own name when activated.

## The problem in one line

Find the single AI-enabled business that can reach **AUD $500,000 revenue (ex GST) between 1 Dec 2027 and 30 Nov 2028**, starting from **zero audience, $500 cash for the first 90 days and 5 founder hours a week**.

## Hard constraints (pass/fail before scoring)

1. **Founder leverage.** 5 hours a week is about 260 hours a year. $500k means roughly **$1,900 revenue per founder hour**. Any model where founders deliver the work, or sell each deal by hand, fails unless the numbers show otherwise.
2. **Cold start.** No network, list or following. Credible acquisition from zero is mandatory. Model zero viral lift.
3. **Validation inside $500.** A paid test must be possible within the cap, ideally before mid-February 2027.
4. **Lawful data.** Authorised APIs, licensed or open data, or permitted access only. No scraping that breaches terms or the Privacy Act 1988.
5. **Automation.** Delivery and support must be mostly automated or delegated to tools after setup, with exceptions handled within the hour budget.

## Scope

- Survey at least **12 candidate models** across product types: software and micro-SaaS, AI agents sold as a service, data products, digital products, marketplaces, and productised services with AI delivery.
- Markets: Australia first, global English-speaking markets where distribution is cheaper.
- Time horizon: evidence from 2025 and 2026; forecasts for 2027 and 2028, clearly labelled.
- Cut to a **shortlist of 3 to 5**, then do deep work on each.

## Specialist assignments

| Assignment | Deliverable | Key questions |
|---|---|---|
| Market and buyer demand | Buyer profile, existing spend, price benchmarks, competitors, 20 named example first customers per finalist | Who already pays to solve this? How much? How do we reach them with no network? |
| Unit economics and ROI | Monthly model, Dec 2027 to Nov 2028, downside, base and upside, plus the prep period | Price, leads, conversion, sales cycle, churn, collection timing, costs, profit, founder hours |
| Growth and distribution | Channel plan from zero, cost per lead estimates, sharing loop design | What gets the first 10 paying customers? What do customers share, and why? |
| Data and automation | Workflow map from lead to support, AI versus rules versus human, tool costs, minutes per customer | What breaks, and who fixes it inside 5 hours a week? |
| Independent challenge | Written attempt to kill the leading option | Why will this fail? What does the incumbent do better? |

## Evidence standards

- Every material claim has a **source and date**, or is marked as an assumption.
- Label each input: **observed**, **assumption** or **forecast (2027/28)**.
- Weight evidence: paid commitments and real spend > buyer behaviour (search volume, job ads, marketplace sales, reviews) > survey intent > social attention.
- Prefer primary sources (pricing pages, public financials, government data, marketplace listings) over commentary.
- Show the arithmetic for every revenue figure.

## Scoring

30% ROI and cash economics, 25% buyer demand and access, 20% automation after setup, 15% speed to paid validation, 10% repeatable distribution (including build-in-public potential for the documentary). Confidence (high, medium, low) is reported separately. Re-rank under conservative assumptions and report whether the leader changes. Ties within 5 points go to the better story.

## Deliverables

1. **Shortlist memo** (3 to 5 finalists with reasons for each cut): by **Wed 2 Dec 2026**.
2. **Recommendation pack** by **Wed 9 Dec 2026**:
   - Ranked scorecard with confidence and sensitivity test
   - Month-by-month model for the leader and the fallback
   - First paid test: offer, buyer criteria, milestone, cost, and pass, revise and stop thresholds
   - Biggest reason it could fail, and the evidence that would trigger a switch to the fallback
   - Open questions for the founders, each tagged with the decision it affects
3. **Story log entries** for key research moments, for the documentary.

All files go in `ll-collective/research/`, with sources in `sources.md`.

## Budget and tools
